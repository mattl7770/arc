# Annotated visual precedent atlas

Date captured: 2026-07-27

## How to use this atlas

These are external visual references, not templates. Each image is paired with
what to transfer and what to reject. CareerPigeon’s current design is not part
of the comparison set.

The useful unit is not “make CareerPigeon look like Linear” or “use Notion’s
illustrations.” It is a specific relationship among hierarchy, density,
content, interaction, and visual character.

## Dashboard and workspace

### Linear My Issues — curated work queue

![Linear My Issues documentation showing a focused grouped work queue](visual-precedents/linear-my-issues.jpg)

Source: [Linear My Issues](https://linear.app/docs/my-issues)

Observe:

- the page states one job and one dominant object;
- tabs separate related scopes without changing the overall model;
- priority appears through ordering and grouping rather than large KPI cards;
- the shell remains quiet while the working list carries density;
- state markers are compact and adjacent to the record.

Transfer:

- make CareerPigeon’s dashboard next-action queue the dominant working region;
- group only around actionable distinctions such as review, due, waiting, and
  changed;
- let secondary progress remain visually quieter.

Reject:

- issue-tracker language, monochrome imitation, extreme compactness for
  first-time users, and keyboard-first assumptions without equivalent visible
  controls.

### Linear Triage — incoming work before active work

![Linear Triage documentation showing a separate intake queue](visual-precedents/linear-triage-docs.jpg)

Source: [Linear Triage](https://linear.app/docs/triage)

Observe:

- intake is a distinct state, not silently merged into active work;
- the central object remains a scan-friendly list;
- navigation shows exactly where triage sits in the larger system;
- actions are conceptually narrow: inspect, accept, decline, duplicate, snooze.

Transfer:

- use this separation for recruiter opportunities and extension-imported jobs;
- preserve context while letting users decide whether an item enters the active
  application system.

Reject:

- treating job opportunities like bug tickets or making automation the visual
  protagonist.

### Asana My Tasks — a legible list with adjacent view controls

![Asana My Tasks showing a structured task list with view tabs, filter, sort, status, priority, and grouping](visual-precedents/asana-my-tasks-list.jpg)

Source: [Asana My Tasks](https://asana.com/features/project-management/my-tasks)

Observe:

- list, timeline, progress, and workload are adjacent views of one working set;
- filter and sort controls sit directly above the records they affect;
- status, priority, date, and project remain compact, aligned properties;
- “Today” and “Recently assigned” create meaningful scan groups without
  fragmenting the records into cards.

Transfer:

- let Applications switch between list and board over one dataset;
- use the same filter/sort language wherever record systems recur;
- keep module-specific default views based on the task;
- preserve visible record context when a user changes views or filters.

Reject:

- the bright generic project-management palette, excessive property columns,
  collaboration counts that do not serve a solo candidate, or exposing
  customization before users understand the default workflow.

### Notion database views — one model, multiple task views

![Notion database views reference showing one database in a structured work surface](visual-precedents/notion-database-views.jpg)

Source: [Notion database views](https://www.notion.com/help/views-filters-and-sorts)

Use this as supporting documentation for the shared-object model, not as a
visual target: the captured help-page frame does not expose enough interface
detail to carry layout decisions. Its transferable point is that view, filter,
sort, and grouping controls can act on one dataset without duplicating records.

## Resume and AI review

### GitHub change review — local evidence and granular decisions

![GitHub documentation showing line-level diff review and contextual commenting](visual-precedents/github-change-review-docs.jpg)

Source: [GitHub change review](https://docs.github.com/en/pull-requests/how-tos/review-pull-requests/reviewing-proposed-changes-in-a-pull-request)

Observe:

- changed and unchanged material remain spatially related;
- comments attach to the smallest meaningful unit;
- review is progressive rather than one all-or-nothing decision;
- the visual language makes additions, removals, and context distinct.

Transfer:

- use claim- or bullet-level changes in the tailored-draft editor;
- attach “why this changed” and source evidence to the changed unit;
- provide accept, edit, reject, and restore without losing document context.

Reject:

- code-review jargon, color-only differences, line-number density, and
  collaboration machinery CareerPigeon does not support.

## Interview practice and feedback

### Prepra active interview — one focused practice moment

![Prepra active interview showing one spoken question, recording state, and a restrained control set](visual-precedents/prepra-active-interview.jpg)

Source: [Prepra](https://www.prepraai.com/)

Observe:

- one question and one speaking task dominate the screen;
- recording status is explicit and adjacent to the session;
- pause and mute are available without turning the experience into a control
  panel;
- the warm typographic framing makes the practice room feel less clinical.

Transfer:

- keep CareerPigeon’s active practice state visually narrow and interruption
  free;
- make listening, recording, paused, and processing states unmistakable;
- keep transcript evidence and deeper coaching outside the live speaking
  focus until the answer is complete.

Reject:

- decorative quotation marks, a generic warm-beige coaching aesthetic, or
  hiding privacy and recording consequences behind a single `REC` label.

### Prepra feedback summary — compact diagnosis, with a critical caveat

![Prepra feedback summary showing rubric rows, score bars, and a short coaching note](visual-precedents/prepra-feedback-summary.jpg)

Source: [Prepra](https://www.prepraai.com/)

Observe:

- feedback is divided into a small number of named dimensions;
- the summary and coach note are visually distinct;
- the page gives the user a clear place to begin reviewing.

Transfer:

- organize feedback into a short diagnostic hierarchy;
- pair each observation with the exact transcript excerpt and a useful retry;
- make “challenge this feedback” and “practice again” first-class actions.

Reject:

- score theater, unexplained precision, feedback detached from transcript
  evidence, or a single composite number that implies more certainty than the
  system has.

## Networking and relationship context

### Dex — person-first history, not a sales pipeline

![Dex showing a person record with relationship context and a people-not-sales-pipeline principle](visual-precedents/dex-relationship-context.jpg)

Source: [Dex](https://getdex.com/)

Observe:

- the person and the relationship note are the primary record;
- the last interaction is visible without a dense activity dashboard;
- the interface explicitly frames the product as relationship memory rather
  than lead management;
- social channels are secondary to human context.

Transfer:

- lead each CareerPigeon networking record with who the person is, how the user
  knows them, recent history, and the next human commitment;
- place a generated message draft after that context, never before it;
- keep reminders user-authored and easy to defer or dismiss.

Reject:

- sales-funnel stages, relationship scores, automated outreach cadence,
  growth-language framing, or generic blue CRM styling.

### Dex reminders — context attached to a concrete follow-up

![Dex reminders showing today, upcoming, and completed follow-ups alongside relationship details](visual-precedents/dex-reminder-history.jpg)

Source: [Dex](https://getdex.com/)

Transfer only the lightweight reminder structure and the connection between a
follow-up and remembered context. Reject birthday automation, “engagement”
optimization, or any signal that CareerPigeon will contact someone without the
user’s explicit review and send action.

## Landing-page directions

### Linear landing — product-led precision

![Linear landing page with restrained typography and product UI entering the first fold](visual-precedents/linear-landing.jpg)

Source: [Linear](https://linear.app/)

Observe:

- one assertive headline carries the opening;
- navigation is quiet and secondary;
- a real product surface enters the first viewport;
- restrained color makes product detail and type do the work;
- the page feels like the product rather than a separate campaign.

Transfer:

- lead with one concrete CareerPigeon outcome;
- bring a truthful connected-workflow artifact close to the first fold;
- use visual restraint to create confidence.

Reject:

- cloning the dark near-black palette, ultra-muted contrast, developer-tool
  severity, or the now-common “Linear aesthetic.”

### Notion product — human editorial character at scale

![Notion product landing page using bold type, illustration, and product imagery](visual-precedents/notion-product-landing.jpg)

Source: [Notion product](https://www.notion.com/product)

Observe:

- illustration has a recognizable authored line and character system;
- typography is bold enough to establish a public identity;
- product imagery, brand art, and social proof are visually differentiated;
- the tone is human despite the AI positioning.

Transfer:

- if CareerPigeon uses illustration, define a real art direction rather than
  isolated generated assets;
- let public typography carry more personality than dense workspace UI;
- connect the human tone to real job-search situations.

Reject:

- the pill embedded in the headline, logo-strip dependence, broad “teams and
  agents” language, or imitation of Notion’s hand-drawn characters.

### Readwise Reader — product artifact as proof and an anti-pattern contrast

![Readwise Reader landing page with a centered hero and large product artifact](visual-precedents/readwise-reader-landing.jpg)

Source: [Readwise Reader](https://readwise.io/read)

Observe:

- the product artifact quickly proves that a real tool exists;
- the headline is audience-specific;
- desktop and mobile product contexts are shown together.

Transfer:

- show CareerPigeon working across the relevant product context;
- make the audience and job explicit.

Reject:

- generic gradient atmosphere, centered hero formula, oversized pill CTA, and
  visual effects that do not explain the product. This reference is included
  partly to calibrate the anti-slop boundary: a strong product screenshot does
  not automatically make the surrounding composition distinctive.

## Blog and editorial system

### Notion Tools & Craft — publication identity

![Notion blog index with editorial masthead, topic rail, authored imagery, and varied story hierarchy](visual-precedents/notion-blog-index.jpg)

Source: [Notion Tools & Craft](https://www.notion.com/blog)

Observe:

- the publication has a name and typographic identity distinct from product
  navigation;
- the topic rail gives the index an editorial structure;
- illustration styles vary in subject but remain recognizably authored;
- story metadata and authorship are visible without dominating;
- the layout creates priority through scale and placement.

Transfer:

- give CareerPigeon’s blog a point of view and a small editorial visual system;
- distinguish feature, foundational guide, and standard story treatments;
- make authorship and topic orientation visible.

Reject:

- copying the script masthead, mascot-like line art, or using a permanent topic
  rail before the content corpus is large enough to justify it.

### Linear Now — searchable product publication

![Linear Now index with topic navigation, search, consistent imagery, and a three-column editorial grid](visual-precedents/linear-now-editorial-index.jpg)

Source: [Linear Now](https://linear.app/now)

Observe:

- category navigation and search appear before the story grid;
- imagery is art-directed into a coherent tonal system;
- titles and summaries carry more weight than tags or marketing labels;
- metadata is quiet but consistently placed.

Transfer:

- use a coherent image treatment and explicit content types;
- make topic browsing useful and visually secondary to stories;
- keep article summaries specific.

Reject:

- a three-card grid as the universal blog structure, low-contrast metadata, or
  dark styling chosen only to match the application.

### Linear long-form article — one strong editorial idea

![Linear long-form article with a restrained title, metadata, and bespoke lead visual](visual-precedents/linear-longform-article.jpg)

Source: [Output isn’t design](https://linear.app/now/output-isn-t-design)

Observe:

- the opening uses one title, minimal metadata, and one bespoke conceptual
  illustration;
- the lead image expresses the article’s argument instead of decorating it;
- the page creates a clear transition from publication navigation to reading.

Transfer:

- commission or direct article visuals around a specific thesis;
- give long-form pieces a calm opening and a readable text column;
- let diagrams and figures explain, not merely brand.

Reject:

- excessive empty space on ordinary how-to articles, dark-mode uniformity, or
  commissioning a bespoke hero for every low-value post.

## Focused forms and onboarding

### GOV.UK question page — clarity before personality

![GOV.UK question-page example with one question, explicit back path, radio choices, and continue action](visual-precedents/govuk-question-page.jpg)

Source: [GOV.UK question pages](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/question-pages/)

Observe:

- one decision dominates;
- labels, targets, back path, and action are unambiguous;
- there is no decorative competition;
- spacing makes the task feel lighter without hiding information.

Transfer:

- use this level of clarity for CareerPigeon onboarding and consequential setup;
- add CareerPigeon character through typography, voice, and supporting context,
  not extra controls.

Reject:

- government-service styling or breaking every low-friction input into a
  separate page when grouping would be faster and equally clear.

## Cross-reference conclusions

The combined direction for CareerPigeon should be:

- Linear’s task focus without the Linear look;
- Asana’s legible record controls without project-management ornament;
- Notion’s authored editorial character without Notion’s visual vocabulary;
- GitHub’s granular review logic without code-review density;
- Prepra’s focused practice room without score theater;
- Dex’s person-first context without CRM behavior;
- GOV.UK’s clarity without government aesthetics;
- product artifacts as proof without the centered-gradient SaaS formula.

That combination is not a mood-board average. It is a set of distinct
principles assigned to distinct CareerPigeon jobs.
